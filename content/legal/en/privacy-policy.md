# GoalsPay Privacy Policy

> This is a translation. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

**Version:** 2026-10-01
**Last updated:** October 1, 2026

This Policy explains what personal data GoalsPay (the "App") processes, for what purpose, with whom it is shared and what rights you have. It applies in accordance with **Ley N.° 787, Ley de Protección de Datos Personales de Nicaragua** (Personal Data Protection Law of Nicaragua), and its regulations. If you live in Costa Rica, you are also protected by **Ley N.° 8968** (Costa Rica's Law on the Protection of Individuals with regard to the Processing of their Personal Data). If you live in another Latin American country, you are protected by your country's personal data law.

## 1. Data controller

**Jefferson José Quezada Irigoyen**, an individual (natural person) domiciled in Granada, Nicaragua.
Privacy contact: **jeffersonirigoyen@gmail.com**

## 2. What data we process

### 2.1 Data you give us

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Email address, first name and last name** | Create and manage your account. |
| **Password** | Sign in. It is stored only as an encrypted *hash* (bcrypt); nobody can read it, not even us. |
| **Financial data you record**: income, fixed and one-off expenses, starting balance, savings goals, contributions, withdrawals, prior savings, categories, dates, amounts and notes | Show you your balance, your goals, your history and your statistics. |
| **Preferences**: currency, language, time zone, theme, colors, reminders, whether biometric lock is turned on | Set up the App the way you chose. |

**You decide what you record.** We do not need your account numbers, cards or banking details, and **we do not ask for them**.

### 2.2 Data generated when you use the App

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Change history** (which goal, income or expense you created, edited or deleted, and when) | Show you the history within the App. |
| **Statistics and achievements** calculated from your own data | Show you your progress. **This is not behavioral tracking** and it is not sent to anyone. |
| **Notification token**: an identifier of your phone used to send you alerts, and its platform (iOS or Android) | Send you the reminders you turned on. |
| **Record of your acceptance** of these documents (version, date and platform) | Prove that you accepted the Terms and this Policy. |

### 2.3 Data we receive if you sign in with another provider

If you choose **Google, Apple, Microsoft or Facebook**, that provider confirms your identity to us and gives us your **email and your name**. We do not receive your password for that service. With Apple you can hide your real email; in that case we receive a forwarding email address from Apple.

### 2.4 What we do NOT collect

- your location (we only use your **time zone** to work out the correct month),
- your contacts, photos, camera or microphone,
- your phone's advertising identifier (IDFA or AAID),
- your fingerprint or your face: biometric unlocking is done by **your phone**, and we never receive that data.

## 3. What we use it for and on what basis

We use your data to:

1. **Provide the service** you asked for: your account, your records, your balance and your goals. The basis is your **consent** and the contractual relationship under the Terms.
2. **Send you notifications**, only if you turn them on. You can turn them off in the App or in your phone's settings.
3. **Protect the App** and your account: prevent unauthorized access and fraud.
4. **Comply with legal obligations**, if a competent authority requires it of us in accordance with the law.

**We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not create advertising profiles.** We do not make automated decisions that produce legal effects on you.

## 4. Who we share it with

Only with providers we need for the App to work, and only with what is strictly necessary:

| Provider | What it receives | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Google, Apple, Microsoft, Meta (Facebook)** | The data from your sign-in with that provider. | Only if you choose to sign in with it. Each one has its own privacy policy. |
| **Expo (650 Industries, USA)**, which in turn uses **Apple Push Notification service** and **Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging** | The notification token and the text of each alert. The text may include the name of a goal or an expense, for example "Your expense *Rent* is due soon". | Only if you turn on notifications. |
| **Cloud infrastructure provider** where our servers and the database run | All of your account data, stored on its servers. | Always, because that is where the service lives. |
| **Apple App Store and Google Play** | The data those stores obtain when you download the App, according to their own policies. | When you download or update the App. |

If you sign in with **Facebook**, Meta's SDK is activated only at that moment. We have **turned off** automatic event logging and the collection of the advertising identifier.

We may also disclose data if a **competent authority** orders it in accordance with the law.

## 5. Transfers outside Nicaragua

Our servers and several of the providers above **may be located in the United States or in other countries**. By accepting this Policy, **you consent to that international transfer**. We only work with providers that apply recognized security measures and that use the data solely to provide their service to us.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **For as long as your account exists.**
- **If you delete your account**, we immediately and permanently delete all of your data from our active database: account, goals, transactions, income, expenses, history, statistics, achievements, tokens and consents. Technical backups, if any, are overwritten in their normal cycle, within a maximum of **30 days**.
- The data stored on **your phone** is deleted when you sign out or uninstall the App. See section 8.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access**: know what data of yours we hold.
- **Rectification**: correct it. You can do this directly in the App.
- **Cancellation or erasure**: delete it. You can do this in the App, under *Settings → Delete account* (*Configuración → Eliminar cuenta* in Spanish).
- **Objection**: object to a processing activity.
- **Portability**: receive your data in a commonly used format.
- **Withdraw your consent** at any time. This does not affect what was done before you withdrew it, but without consent we cannot continue providing the service to you.

**How to exercise them:**

1. Write to **jeffersonirigoyen@gmail.com** from your account's email address.
2. State which right you want to exercise.
3. We will reply within a maximum of **5 business days**.
4. We may ask you to confirm your identity before replying.

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can contact the authority in your country:

- **Nicaragua:** the Dirección de Protección de Datos Personales (Personal Data Protection Directorate).
- **Costa Rica:** the Agencia de Protección de Datos de los Habitantes (PRODHAB) (Agency for the Protection of Inhabitants' Data).
- **Other countries:** the relevant personal data authority.

## 8. What is stored on your phone

The App does not use *cookies*. It does store on your phone the minimum information it needs to work:

| What | Where | Purpose | When it is deleted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your session: access tokens | The system's encrypted storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android) | So you don't have to sign in every time. | When you sign out or uninstall. |
| Summary for the widget: total saved, number of goals and currency | The App's local storage | Show the widget on your home screen. | When you sign out or uninstall. |
| Which achievements you have already been shown | The App's local storage | Not repeat the same celebration. | When you sign out or uninstall. |
| Provider session (Google or Facebook), if you used it | The provider's storage on your phone | Sign in with that provider. | When you sign out of GoalsPay. |

## 9. Security

- Communication between the App and our servers **is encrypted in transit (HTTPS)**.
- Passwords are stored as a *hash* and never in readable text.
- Your session is stored in your phone's encrypted storage.
- You can turn on **fingerprint or face lock** to open the App.
- Our technical logs do not store passwords, full tokens or your financial data.

No system is 100% secure. If an incident affecting your data occurs, we will notify you, and we will notify the authority when the law requires it.

## 10. Minors

GoalsPay is **only for people aged 18 or over**. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we detect an account belonging to a minor, we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

If we change this Policy in a significant way, **we will notify you in the App and ask you to accept it again**. The current version and date appear at the top of this document.
