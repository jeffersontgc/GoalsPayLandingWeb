# Cookie and Analytics Policy for the goalspay.app website

> This is a translation. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

**Version:** 2026-10-01
**Last updated:** October 1, 2026

This Policy applies **only to the goalspay.app website**. The GoalsPay mobile app **does not use cookies or analytics**. What the App stores on your phone is explained in section 8 of the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## 1. What cookies are

They are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information between visits. Your browser's *local storage* works in a similar way, and this Policy covers it too.

## 2. What goalspay.app stores in your browser

Only what is **strictly necessary** for the site to work. **We do not use advertising, social media or cross-site tracking cookies.**

| What | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Strictly necessary cookie | Remember the site's language: the one you chose or, on your first visit, your browser's (Spanish or English). | Up to 1 year |
| `theme` | Local storage, not a cookie | Remember whether you chose the light or dark theme. | Until you delete it |

These do not require your consent, because without them the site does not work as you expect. They do not let us identify you.

## 3. Site analytics

To know how many people visit the site and how fast it loads, we use two services from **Vercel Inc.** (USA), the company that hosts the site:

| Service | What it measures | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Vercel Web Analytics** | Pages visited, the site you came from, country, device type, operating system and browser. | **No cookies.** It counts visits with a temporary identifier computed from the request, which is discarded within **24 hours** at most. It does not build a profile of you and does not follow you to other sites. |
| **Vercel Speed Insights** | Page load times and performance (*Core Web Vitals*), along with the device type and connection. | **No cookies.** Technical performance metrics only. |

In both cases:

- **We only see aggregate figures**, for example "120 visits to the home page this week". We do not see who you are.
- **We do not use them for advertising** or combine them with the data in your App account.
- Vercel handles this information **as our service provider**, only to provide this service to us, and may process it in the United States or other countries.

Because they use no cookies and do not identify you, they work without asking for your consent. You can still avoid them:

- by blocking requests to `/_vercel/insights` and `/_vercel/speed-insights` in your browser or with a content blocker, or
- by using private browsing mode.

The site works exactly the same if you block them.

## 4. If this changes

If we ever add cookies or tools that are not strictly necessary, or that identify you:

- **we will ask for your consent before turning them on**, with a notice that lets you accept or reject them just as easily;
- we will update this Policy to say what they are, who sets them and for how long.

## 5. How to control them

You can delete cookies and local storage from your browser's settings. If you delete them, the site will go back to the default language and theme.
