# Delete your GoalsPay account

> This is a translation. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

**Version:** 2026-10-01
**Last updated:** October 1, 2026

You can delete your GoalsPay account and all of its data at any time. **This cannot be undone.**

## 1. From the App

1. Open GoalsPay and sign in.
2. Go to *Settings → My profile → Delete account* (*Configuración → Mi perfil → Eliminar cuenta* in Spanish).
3. Follow the instructions to confirm the deletion.

## 2. If you don't have access to the App

Write to **jeffersonirigoyen@gmail.com** **from your GoalsPay account's email address** and say that you want to delete your account.

- If you signed up with Apple and hid your email, include in the message the Apple forwarding email address your account uses.
- We may ask you to confirm your identity before deleting it.
- We will reply within a maximum of **5 business days**.

## 3. What is deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently delete all of your data from our active database:

- your account: email, first name, last name, password and preferences;
- your goals, transactions, income and expenses;
- your history, your statistics and your achievements;
- your tokens, including the notification token;
- the record of your consents.

The data stored on **your phone** is deleted when you sign out or uninstall the App.

Deleting your GoalsPay account does not delete your Google, Apple, Microsoft or Facebook account.

## 4. When it is deleted

| Where | Timing |
|---|---|
| Active database | **Immediately.** |
| Technical backups, if any | Overwritten in their normal cycle, within a maximum of **30 days**. |

If you ask by email, these timings start when we confirm your request.

More details in sections 6 and 7 of the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
